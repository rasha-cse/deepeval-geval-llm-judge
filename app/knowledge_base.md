# Acme Cloud Storage – Knowledge Base

## Plans
- Free: 5 GB storage, 1 user, community support.
- Pro: $9 per user per month, 1 TB storage per user, email support within 24 hours.
- Business: $25 per user per month, unlimited storage, 24/7 phone support, SSO.

## Billing
- Plans are billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing saves 20%.
- You can cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the paid period.
- Refunds are available within 14 days of the first payment only.

## Files
- Maximum single file size is 50 GB on Pro and Business, 2 GB on Free.
- Deleted files stay in the Trash for 30 days before permanent deletion.

## Security
- Files are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- Two-factor authentication is available on all plans.
